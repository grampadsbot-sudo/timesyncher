# Jev quality, post-intake welcome, and Thing pages

SoT: `bot-admin/messages/time-syncher/dialog-jev-quality-rewrite-and-post-intake-20260926`.

Hold certify. This inventory is not a certify.

## Customer path

1. **Onboarding.** After Agree, `vacation-app.html` shows the onboarding chat (`data-screen="onboarding"`). The itinerary is the shared app at `/shared/intake-…`, not Onboarding/Itinerary cards.
2. **Every generated reply.** `produceLiveAppReply` classifies with Jev, then the bake-off tier writes a draft. Jev (`typesafe/jev-1.13`) only scores that draft and returns a one-line fix note for that turn. Jev does not write the rewrite. A score of 4 or 5 ships the draft only when the note does not name a defect. A score of 3 or lower, a defect note at any score, or an invented place asks the same-tier model (T1 `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite`, T2 `qwen/qwen3-235b-a22b-2507`, T3 `deepseek/deepseek-v3.2`, T4 `qwen/qwen3-max`) to rewrite from the note, then Jev scores the rewrite. The customer always gets a reply. While the rewrite runs, the interim reply is always generated by T1 `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite` from the customer's actual turn. It is never a template or a canned string, and there is no template fallback. If T1 is slow, the typing indicator covers the wait. The turn logs `interimReply.text`, `interimReply.model`, and `interimReply.ms`. A template interim, or the same interim text on two turns, fails verification. The rewrite ships when its score is higher. Otherwise the higher-scoring text ships with `flagged: true`. The Dialog PDF label is `rewritten by <tier model> (Jev note)`. The customer app does not show the score. Each generated turn logs `draftModel`, `rewriteModel`, `shippedModel`, `jevScoreDraft`, `jevScoreRewrite`, `jevNote`, `interimReply` (text, model, ms), `latencyMs` (draft, rewrite, total), and `flagged`. A near-identical rewrite, a template note, a repeated or template interim, or a customer turn with no reply fails verification. A price question names `$27` and `unlimited vacations for the whole year`. A reply with no dollar price scores 3 or lower. The note `Keep the reply. It answers "..."` is a template and fails.
3. **Long intake.** The first customer dump of about seventy words that names the trip (place plus gardens, swim, groceries, dinner, family, or dates) is the post-intake turn. That reply says the itinerary is being built from the dump, explains view access and edit access, says people join from the approved email invite, and gives the one unlimited upsell in that same reply. The later price question ships `$27`.
4. **Itinerary and Thing pages.** That same dump stores Things from the customer's words: Big Island, Gardens, Groceries, Dinner, Swim, and the Kailua-Kona house when those words are in the dump. The trip badge leaves `no vacations yet` and shows the place and dates the customer named, such as Big Island Apr 3–12 2026. Itinerary (`data-screen="itinerary"`) lists each Thing with that day placement: the trip span, groceries on the arrival day with the airport shuttle, a later-week swim, and the no-two-big-activities rule. Each Thing page (`data-screen="thing"`) shows who, when, the intake notes, and a collaborator note surface. Swim when-line keeps the intake "later in the week", then the customer's Monday beach or house pool. A second swim day is added only when the customer named that weekday. An app-only Tuesday or Thursday does not become a when chip. Later customer lines that name a day update that Thing's day. They do not invent a place. Groceries stay on arrival day, Fri Apr 3. A garden Thing is titled Gardens. The producer does not invent Kahaluu, Pua Mau, an arboretum, a snorkel cruise, Volcanoes, Puʻuhonua o Hōnaunau, Captain Cook, or a weather excuse that moves the garden.
5. **Rewrite proof.** When the tier model's rewrite is the customer-facing reply, the stored turn keeps `quality.rewritten` true, `quality.draft` as the text the customer did not see, and `rewriteModel` as that tier model. The transcript body is the rewrite. A rewrite that does not score higher stays off the customer reply and `flagged` is true. An unchanged or near-identical rewrite does not count as rewritten.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the app URL and agree to the terms. That is Onboarding.
- Send the long trip dump. Read the reply that says the itinerary is being built and names the household plan.
- Choose Itinerary, then open each Thing. Those shell cards are not the Feature Map itinerary. A shot of them fails `features/dialog-screenshot-gate.md`.
