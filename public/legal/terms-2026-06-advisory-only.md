# TimeSyncher Terms and Privacy Acknowledgment

Status: product/legal workbench language, not final legal advice. Before customer-facing use, IBE Inc. should have counsel review it.

## Purpose

These terms make the customer responsibilities, TimeSyncher service limits, and acceptance record explicit before the customer starts TimeSyncher Vacation onboarding.

## 1. Parties and service

TimeSyncher is provided by or on behalf of IBE Inc. TimeSyncher Vacation uses AI systems, software tools, Telegram intake, hosted itinerary pages, payment receipts, and support workflows to help customers plan and organize travel.

## 2. Advisory-only service

For the current TimeSyncher Vacation release, TimeSyncher is advisory-only. It may research, compare, summarize, draft, organize, and recommend.

TimeSyncher is not authorized to take higher-authority actions for the customer. It cannot book, buy, pay, send messages to vendors, modify email, modify calendars, cancel or reschedule reservations, accept third-party terms, change accounts, or commit the customer to an external obligation.

If a customer asks for one of those actions, TimeSyncher may prepare information or a draft, but the customer must complete the action themselves.

## 3. Access and OAuth policy

No Google OAuth access is required for the current TimeSyncher Vacation onboarding step. TimeSyncher should not request write scopes for Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts, or other external accounts unless the product later ships a separately reviewed delegated-action flow.

## 4. AI and information risk

TimeSyncher uses AI systems to interpret requests and produce planning assistance. AI outputs may be incomplete, stale, incorrect, or based on third-party information that changes without notice.

Customers are responsible for checking important details before acting, including dates, prices, availability, passport or visa requirements, policies, cancellation terms, safety considerations, and suitability for travelers.

## 5. Third-party services

TimeSyncher depends on third-party services such as AI model providers, Telegram, hosting/runtime providers, payment processors, mapping/search sources, and travel vendors. IBE Inc. does not control those third-party services and is not responsible for their outages, bugs, pricing, policies, data handling, or availability changes.

## 6. Customer responsibilities

The customer is responsible for providing accurate contact and trip information, reviewing TimeSyncher outputs before relying on them, making any bookings or payments themselves, and promptly reporting unwanted behavior or support issues.

## 7. No emergency or high-risk use

TimeSyncher is not an emergency service, medical/legal/financial decision-maker, or replacement for urgent human judgment.

## 8. Acceptable use

The customer must not use TimeSyncher for illegal, abusive, deceptive, harassing, discriminatory, privacy-invasive, security-invasive, or unauthorized activity.

## 9. Modification, suspension, and termination

IBE Inc. may modify, limit, suspend, or terminate TimeSyncher functionality if required for safety, security, compliance, cost control, technical integrity, or operational reasons.

## 10. Versioning and acceptance

The onboarding record stores the accepted terms version, accepted timestamp, accepted-by name, text hash, capability/access snapshot hash, and receipt hash. The backend receipt is the authoritative acceptance record.
