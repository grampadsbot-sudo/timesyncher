FAQ

Key questions.

Is this another travel-planning app?

No. TimeSyncher Vacation is a one-of-a-kind AI vacation assistant that helps your family unlock unforgettable experiences. It helps you decide what matters most, compare the real tradeoffs, budget the whole vacation, and protect the moments you will actually remember.

Who is it for?

Couples, families, groups, and multi-generational travelers who want a one-of-a-kind vacation without turning vacation planning into a second job.

Does TimeSyncher book travel for me?

No. The app helps organize and compare choices so you can book confidently.

What happens to my travel data?

We don’t sell your personal travel data or use it for anything unrelated to your vacation. We only use it to provide and support your plan. See the Privacy Policy for details.
