Privacy Policy

TimeSyncher Vacation (“TimeSyncher,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides consumer travel-planning software that helps customers organize vacation ideas, preferences, reservations, budgets, daily plans, and related communications.

Information we collect

We may collect account and contact information, vacation details, traveler preferences, vacation-planning items, uploaded or forwarded travel materials, support messages, device/browser information, payment-related records from payment processors, and usage data needed to operate and improve the service.

How we use information

We use information to provide the TimeSyncher Vacation app, create and maintain vacation plans, support customers, secure the service, process purchases or subscriptions, communicate about the service, comply with legal obligations, and improve product quality using aggregated or de-identified patterns where practical.

Travel data and sensitive choices

Vacation plans may include personal preferences, locations, dates, companions, dietary notes, mobility needs, budgets, and reservation details. Please only add information that you are authorized to share with the travelers in your family vacation.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information or use personal travel details for purposes unrelated to providing and supporting the service. We may share information with service providers that help us host, operate, analyze, support, secure, and process payments for the service. We may also share information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transaction.

AI-assisted features

TimeSyncher Vacation may use AI systems to summarize, organize, compare, and generate travel-planning content. We use reasonable safeguards and vendor controls, but AI output should be reviewed before booking, purchasing, or relying on time-sensitive travel details.

Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, and improve the product. Customers may request deletion subject to lawful retention needs.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or portability where applicable. You may unsubscribe from non-essential marketing communications using available unsubscribe mechanisms or by contacting support.

Children

The service is intended for adults purchasing or managing travel plans. It is not directed to children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No online service can guarantee perfect security.
